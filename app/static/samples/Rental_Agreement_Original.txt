RESIDENTIAL RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is made on 1st April 2025 at Chennai, Tamil Nadu, between:

Mr. R. Venkatesh, residing at 14 Lake View Road, Chennai 600017 (hereinafter called the "LANDLORD"),

AND

Ms. Priya Sharma, residing at Flat 3B, Green Park Apartments, 22 Anna Nagar Main Road, Chennai 600040 (hereinafter called the "TENANT").

1. PREMISES
The Landlord agrees to let out Flat 3B, Green Park Apartments, 22 Anna Nagar Main Road, Chennai 600040, a 2BHK residential flat with one covered car parking slot (the "Premises"), to the Tenant for residential use only.

2. TERM
The tenancy shall be for a period of eleven (11) months commencing from 1st April 2025 and ending on 28th February 2026. The first eleven (11) months shall be a lock-in period during which the Tenant cannot terminate this Agreement.

3. RENT
3.1 The monthly rent is Rs. 25,000 (Rupees Twenty-Five Thousand only), payable in advance on or before the 5th day of every English calendar month.
3.2 If the rent is not paid by the due date, the Tenant shall pay a late fee of 5% of the monthly rent for every day of delay.
3.3 On renewal, the rent shall automatically increase by 15% without any further notice.

4. SECURITY DEPOSIT
4.1 The Tenant has paid an interest-free security deposit of Rs. 2,50,000 (Rupees Two Lakh Fifty Thousand only), equal to ten months' rent.
4.2 Rs. 25,000 out of the security deposit is non-refundable and shall be retained by the Landlord towards painting and cleaning charges, irrespective of the condition of the Premises.
4.3 The balance deposit shall be refunded within 90 days after the Tenant vacates, after deducting any amount the Landlord, in his sole discretion, considers necessary.

5. TERMINATION BY NOTICE
After the lock-in period, either party may terminate this Agreement by giving one (1) month's written notice to the other party.

6. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS
6.1 The Tenant shall bear the cost of all repairs to the Premises, including structural repairs, plumbing, electrical wiring and water-proofing, whether or not the damage was caused by the Tenant.
6.2 Monthly society maintenance charges of Rs. 3,000 shall be paid by the Tenant directly to the association.

7. UTILITIES
Electricity and water charges shall be paid by the Tenant as per actual meter readings.

8. ENTRY AND INSPECTION
The Landlord or his agents may enter and inspect the Premises at any time, with or without notice to the Tenant.

9. SUB-LETTING
The Tenant shall not sub-let, assign or part with possession of the Premises or any part of it.

10. EARLY EXIT
If the Tenant vacates the Premises before the expiry of the lock-in period for any reason, the entire security deposit shall be forfeited by the Landlord.

11. VACATING THE PREMISES
The Tenant must give the Landlord three (3) months' prior written notice before vacating the Premises. On vacating, the Tenant shall hand over the Premises in the same condition as at the start of the tenancy, and repaint the entire flat at her own cost.

12. PETS AND ALTERATIONS
No pets are allowed. The Tenant shall not make any alterations to the Premises without the Landlord's prior written consent.

13. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
Any dispute arising under this Agreement shall be referred to a sole arbitrator appointed by the Landlord. The decision of the arbitrator shall be final and binding. The courts at Chennai shall have exclusive jurisdiction.

14. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first written above.

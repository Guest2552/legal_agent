RESIDENTIAL RENTAL AGREEMENT (REVISED DRAFT)

This Rental Agreement is made on 1st April 2025 at Chennai, Tamil Nadu, between:

Mr. R. Venkatesh, residing at 14 Lake View Road, Chennai 600017 (the "LANDLORD"),

AND

Ms. Priya Sharma, residing at Flat 3B, Green Park Apartments, 22 Anna Nagar Main Road, Chennai 600040 (the "TENANT").

1. PREMISES
The Landlord agrees to let out Flat 3B, Green Park Apartments, 22 Anna Nagar Main Road, Chennai 600040, a 2BHK residential flat with one covered car parking slot (the "Premises"), to the Tenant for residential use only.

2. TERM
The tenancy shall be for a period of eleven (11) months commencing from 1st April 2025 and ending on 28th February 2026. The first six (6) months shall be a lock-in period.

3. RENT
3.1 The monthly rent is Rs. 25,000 (Rupees Twenty-Five Thousand only), payable in advance on or before the 5th day of every English calendar month.
3.2 If the rent is not paid within 10 days of the due date, the Tenant shall pay simple interest at 12% per annum on the overdue amount.
3.3 On renewal, the rent may be revised by mutual written agreement, subject to a maximum increase of 5%.

4. SECURITY DEPOSIT
4.1 The Tenant has paid an interest-free security deposit of Rs. 50,000 (Rupees Fifty Thousand only), equal to two months' rent.
4.2 The security deposit shall be refunded in full within 30 days after the Tenant vacates and hands over the keys, after deducting only unpaid rent, unpaid utility bills and the reasonable cost of repairing damage caused by the Tenant beyond normal wear and tear. The Landlord shall give the Tenant an itemised statement with receipts for every deduction.

5. TERMINATION BY NOTICE
After the lock-in period, either party may terminate this Agreement by giving one (1) month's written notice to the other party.

6. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS
6.1 The Landlord shall be responsible for structural repairs, water-proofing, plumbing and electrical wiring, and for any damage not caused by the Tenant.
6.2 The Tenant shall be responsible for minor day-to-day repairs such as replacing bulbs, taps and switches, and for damage caused by the Tenant or her guests.
6.3 Monthly society maintenance charges of Rs. 3,000 shall be paid by the Tenant directly to the association.

7. UTILITIES
Electricity and water charges shall be paid by the Tenant as per actual meter readings.

8. ENTRY AND INSPECTION
The Landlord may inspect the Premises between 9 a.m. and 7 p.m. after giving at least 24 hours' prior written notice to the Tenant, except in an emergency.

9. SUB-LETTING
The Tenant shall not sub-let, assign or part with possession of the Premises or any part of it without the Landlord's written consent.

10. EARLY EXIT
If the Tenant vacates the Premises during the lock-in period, the Tenant shall pay rent only for the remaining months of the lock-in period or until a new tenant approved by the Landlord moves in, whichever is earlier. The security deposit shall be refunded as per Clause 4.2.

11. VACATING THE PREMISES
On vacating, the Tenant shall hand over the Premises in the same condition as at the start of the tenancy, subject to normal wear and tear. A joint inspection shall be carried out on the day of handover and signed by both parties.

12. PETS AND ALTERATIONS
Small pets are allowed with prior written intimation to the Landlord. The Tenant shall not make any structural alterations to the Premises without the Landlord's prior written consent.

13. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
The parties shall first try to resolve any dispute through mutual discussion for 30 days. If unresolved, either party may approach the Rent Authority or the competent civil court at Chennai.

14. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India.
